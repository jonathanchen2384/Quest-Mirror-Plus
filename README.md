# Quest-Mirror-Plus
Welcome!
This is an App for screen casting easily to your headset!

To screen cast, follow these steps:

WIRED METHOD:
1. Plug in your USB to your headset
2. A pop-up will be in your headset to trust your device. Select "Always Allow"
3. Now open up the app, then click "Set Up / Repair Wireless Connection"
4. Once you get your IP Address, click "Connect Wirelessly"

You can now unplug your USB.

5. Once you're done, click "Start VR Mirror"


WIRELESS METHOD:
1. Enter your IP Address (Found in the headset's currently connected Wifi Network)
2. Click "Connect Wirelessly"
3. Click "Start VR Mirror".


That's it!


To Exit fullscreen:

MacOS: COMMAND + F (Or F11)


Windows: Alt + F (Or F11)
